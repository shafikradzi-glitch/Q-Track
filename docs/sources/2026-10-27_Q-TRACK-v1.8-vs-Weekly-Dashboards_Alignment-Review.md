# Q-TRACK v1.8 vs Weekly Project Dashboards: Content Alignment Review

rebuilt the Content only (what is highlighted to management), not visual design · Q-TRACK Solution Documentation Pack v1.8 (22 Oct 2026) against the QA Portfolio Command Centre (week 17–23 Oct 2026)

2026-10-27 · Shafik Radzi


## 1. Summary

The proposed dashboards highlight the right problems but present them from the wrong level. Almost everything they show is Level 2 project and contractor performance, while Q-TRACK v1.8 requires management to see HQ QA's own Level 4 conclusions first, with project performance kept separate and labelled as such (DR-02, DR-03).

**Five things that must change before the dashboards go to management**

1. **Lead with the Level 4 assurance position, not a composite index.** Q-TRACK's headline is each project's ten-area assessment (Green / Amber / Red / Grey, overall = worst area). The dashboards have no equivalent; their health index is built from contractor counts.
2. **Label project data as project performance.** NCR closure, RFI, WIR and submission codes are Level 2 records owned by projects and contractors. Q-TRACK never shows them as QA output, and never makes HQ QA the owner of contractor NCR closure.
3. **Replace NCR closure rate with Q-TRACK's NCR measures.** Q-TRACK judges NCR management by ageing past expected completion (30 / 60 / 90 days), median age, repeat rate, systemic signals and CAPA verified effective, not by a cumulative closure percentage.
4. **Add the HQ QA work Q-TRACK puts on the executive dashboard.** Coverage by tier against the 90% target, open HQ QA findings by severity, effectiveness checks, escalations (levels A–C), evidence requests, tier triggers and critical milestone schedule variance are all missing.
5. **Use the Q-TRACK workbook load as the L4/L5 source.** The 21 Oct load resolves the conflicting slide totals (for example 782 RFIs, 2,348 submissions, 36 of 80 NCR/OBS open) and surfaces issues the slides miss, such as only 8 of 40 on-going projects having an approved PQP.

**Alignment by page**

| Page | Aligned with Q-TRACK | Main gap | Conflict to fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portfolio | Decisions required; data-as-at dates; method note | No Level 4 heat-map, no coverage, no key alerts | Composite index treats unreported inputs as 50 and mixes Level 2 data |
| Penang Mutiara Line | Critical issue (SWO) and decisions | No HQ QA findings, escalation level or systemic issue for the temperature NCRs | Decision wording directs contractors, which is not HQ QA's role |
| RTS Link | NCR ageing; handover risk | Milestone schedule variance for CPC dates; CAPA effectiveness | NCR closure % presented as a QA KPI |
| L4/L5 Projects | Name now matches DR-01; data-quality watchlist | PQP coverage, overdue OIL and RFIs, recurrence by contractor | Slide totals used where the Q-TRACK load gives the reconciled figures |

The dashboards remain useful as the weekly project-level view. Section 7 sets out exactly what each page should highlight once realigned.


## 2. The Q-TRACK reference: what management must see

Q-TRACK v1.8 defines management content in two places that agree with each other: the executive dashboard (Part 9, Section 1) and the monthly management assurance report (RMM §21, Part 7). Both are built on HQ QA's role as Level 4: it assures the effectiveness of Levels 1–3, does not act as contractor QC, does not issue contractor NCRs, and does not own or close contractor corrective actions (Part 1, Section 1).


### 2.1 Executive dashboard content (v1.8)

| Band | What is highlighted | Method stated on the chart |
|---|---|---|
| Key alerts | Open Critical and High alerts from Projects and Assurance assessment, each with project and escalation date; data freshness | Rule-based (e.g. NCR ageing over 30 / 60 / 90 days, assessment overdue, effectiveness check due) |
| Projects | Active projects by type and tier; provisional tiers; critical milestones at risk and due in 30 days; L4/L5 workbook load date | Stacked count by programme and tier; schedule variance = forecast − baseline |
| Assurance assessment | Level 4 position (Red / Amber / Green / Grey counts); projects needing attention; coverage by tier; area status across the ten areas; open HQ QA findings by severity; effectiveness checks; escalations; evidence requests | Coverage = done ÷ due, target 90%; cross-portfolio distribution of area status |
| NCR & CAPA | Open NCR/OBS; % past expected completion; number over 90 days; median age; CAPA verified effective; repeat rate; systemic signals | c-chart with Mann–Kendall trend; backlog trend; root-cause Pareto; ageing bands; Wilson 95% interval (green only if lower bound ≥ 80%) |


### 2.2 Monthly management assurance report (six sections)

1. Overall assurance conclusion: status, confidence, three reasons, limitations.
2. Portfolio dashboard: the frozen heat-map.
3. Top five quality risks.
4. Significant findings, overdue actions and escalations.
5. NCR / CAPA trends, labelled as project performance.
6. Management decisions required.


### 2.3 Rules that govern what may be shown

| Rule | Source | What it means for a dashboard |
|---|---|---|
| L4/L5 is the project category; Level 4 is the HQ QA assurance level | DR-01 | Use "L4/L5 Projects" for the portfolio; never call project data "Level 4" |
| Level 2 counts are project performance, never HQ QA output | DR-02 | Banner or label on every figure drawn from project registers |
| HQ QA does not own project NCRs | DR-03 | Show NCR status as the project's; HQ QA shows its adequacy and effectiveness assessment beside it |
| No personal results on the executive view | DR-07 | Officer KPIs and GPS goals stay on the Performance dashboard |
| Grey is never shown as Green | RMM §9 | Insufficient evidence is a status in its own right |
| Missing data is excluded, not scored | Part 8 §3 | Weight is redistributed; below 60% of weight with data, the result is "Insufficient data" |
| Unapproved thresholds are Provisional | Part 8; RMM Annex A | Targets and bands carry a Proposed or Provisional label |
| Every chart states its method; every figure traces to records | Part 9 §1 | A Method line under each chart; source and load date on each figure |
| Contractor KPI figures are restricted | Part 4 §9 | Visible to Head of QA and the admin clerk only |

## 4. Conflicts with Q-TRACK rules

Eight items in the proposed dashboards contradict a Q-TRACK rule. The first three would draw challenge in a management meeting, because they blur who owns contractor performance.

| # | What the dashboard shows | Q-TRACK rule | Why it matters | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | A Quality Health Index per project, weighted from NCR closure, inspection closure, Code 1 rate and audit | Overall Level 4 result = worst applicable area of ten (RMM §9); Level 2 counts are never HQ QA output (DR-02) | A composite averages a Red area away; a contractor's counts become QA's score | Replace with the ten-area assessment. If an index is kept, label it "Project quality indicator (Level 2 data)" and apply rule X2 |
| X2 | Unreported inputs scored a neutral 50 | Missing components are excluded and weight redistributed; under 60% of weight with data = Insufficient data (Part 8 §3) | A 50 invents evidence; Q-TRACK forbids it | Apply the exclusion rule; show configured and effective weight (section 5) |
| X3 | NCR closure % against an 80% target as a headline KPI | NCR management is Green when no Major / Critical NCR is overdue and ageing is within bands (RMM §9.1 area 4); closure rate is not a Q-TRACK measure | Closure % rewards old NCRs being closed late and hides overdue ones | Show % past expected completion, number over 90 days, median age |
| X4 | Decisions such as "instruct contractor to clear 282 RFIs" and "set contractor deadlines to close 35 NCR/OBS" | HQ QA does not own or close contractor corrective actions; it escalates (levels A–C) and raises findings or systemic issues (Part 1 §1; Part 5) | HQ QA appears to take on Level 2 work (KPI goal R1-6 penalises this) | Word each as an HQ QA escalation to the Project Director, citing the escalation level and the HQ QA finding |
| X5 | "Close the 9 open external audit findings" assigned to the RTS QA Assistant Manager | The responsible party owns the corrective action; QA monitors, assesses adequacy and verifies effectiveness (Part 5) | Assigns contractor closure to a QA officer | Owner = audited contractor; QA action = verify effectiveness by the check date |
| X6 | Targets of 80%, 85%, 70% shown as settled | All Level 4 thresholds are Proposed until Head of QA approves them (RMM Annex A) | Readers take a Proposed target as policy | Mark every target Proposed or Provisional |
| X7 | Engagement counts as a headline measure (L4/L5) | Engagements are workbook context, not on the executive dashboard; HQ-8 and OPS measure delivery and acceptance | Activity reads as impact | Move to the L4/L5 register view; keep off the management page |
| X8 | QA personnel and their package allocation on project pages | DR-07: no personal results on the executive view | Names beside performance figures read as personal attribution | Keep the QA lead as a contact line only; no figures beside names |

None of these require new data. They are corrections of definition and wording that can be made before the next issue.


## 5. The composite index under Q-TRACK rules

Applying Q-TRACK's missing-data rule turns RTS Link and L4/L5 green, yet both carry control weaknesses that Q-TRACK's worst-area rule would rate Red. This is the clearest evidence that the composite index should not be the headline.

| Project | As built (unreported = 50) | Q-TRACK missing-data rule (weight with data) | Indicative worst-area result* | Area driving the result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Penang Mutiara Line | 29 Red | 20 Red (70%) | Red | Area 3 QC effectiveness, 4 NCR management, 5 CAPA effectiveness: 37 pile caps cast after the first NCR; SWO in force |
| RTS Link | 79 Amber | 82 Green (90%) | Red | Area 4 NCR management: 126 NCRs more than a month overdue, Safety the largest category; area 10 handover: 64% of ACP drawings under review |
| L4/L5 Projects | 69 Amber | 81 Green (60%); 77 Amber on the 21 Oct Q-TRACK load | Red or Amber | Area 1 Project QMS / PQP: 8 of 40 on-going projects have a PQP; area 4: 9 NCR/OBS past expected completion by up to 355 days |

*Indicative only, from the evidence in the slides and the Q-TRACK load. It is not an approved assessment: Q-TRACK requires an assessor, an independent validator and Head of QA approval, and Grey where evidence is insufficient.

The L4/L5 figure falls from 81 to 77 simply by switching from slide figures to the Q-TRACK load (RFI closure 78.5% instead of 86%; Code 1 49% instead of 51.9%; NCR/OBS closure 55.0% instead of 56.3%). A score that moves on data source alone should not head a management page.

**Recommendation.** Headline each project with its latest approved Level 4 overall status and the area that sets it. Keep project indicators (closure, Code 1, inspection closure) in a clearly labelled Level 2 panel, scored, if at all, under the Part 8 missing-data rule with configured and effective weights shown.

## 8. What the dashboards add, and gaps in Q-TRACK itself

The comparison runs both ways. The weekly dashboards carry four things Q-TRACK v1.8 does not, and the review exposed five gaps Q-TRACK should close before go-live.


### 8.1 Keep: content the dashboards add

| Item | Why keep it | Where it fits in Q-TRACK |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly cadence for PML and RTS | Q-TRACK's management report is monthly; the SWO and NCR ageing move weekly | Executive dashboard filtered to the week; key alerts |
| Critical-issue banner with lift conditions (PML SWO) | Gives management the single item that stops work | Escalation register level C, with conditions as linked actions |
| Reporting-defect watchlist for PML and RTS slides | Q-TRACK's data-quality report covers only the L4/L5 workbook | Extend the data-quality report to the PML / RTS imports |

## 9. Actions and decisions for Head of QA

Three decisions unlock most of the realignment; the rest are wording and data-source changes the team can make in the next weekly cycle.

**Decisions**

1. Retire the composite index as the headline, or keep it only as a labelled Level 2 indicator under the Part 8 missing-data rule.
2. Adopt Q-TRACK's NCR measures (ageing bands, median age, repeat, systemic, CAPA effective) in place of NCR closure rate across all three projects.
3. Make the Q-TRACK L4/L5 load the only source for the L4/L5 page, and decide whether to extend imports to PML and RTS submissions and inspections (gap Q1).

**Actions**

- Reword every decision and risk so HQ QA escalates and verifies, and the project or contractor owns closure (X4, X5)
- Mark all targets and bands Proposed until thresholds are approved (X6)
- Add the overall assurance conclusion with confidence and limitations to each page (report §1)
- Raise the PML temperature NCRs as an HQ QA finding and test them against the repeat rule; log the SWO as a level C escalation
- Record RTS CPC and handover dates as critical milestones so schedule variance can be shown
- Put PQP coverage (8 of 40), overdue NCR/OBS and stalled RFIs at the top of the L4/L5 page
- Move engagement counts and activity lists off the management pages (X7)
- Complete the first approved Level 4 assessments so the heat-map can replace the index
